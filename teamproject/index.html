Simple rules. Sharper thinking.

  1. Reveal two cards. Matching symbols stay open.
  2. Consecutive matches earn a combo bonus: 100, 125, 150, 175, then 200 points per pair. A mismatch resets the streak.
  3. Peek reveals all cards for one second. It costs 50 points, with a minimum score of zero. Hints are limited.
  4. Timed mode: finish before the clock reaches zero. The timer continues in background tabs.
  5. Duel: a match keeps your turn; a mismatch passes it. Most pairs wins. Practice and duel have no timer.
